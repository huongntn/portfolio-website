About Me

I am a software developer based in Heilbronn, Germany. My work revolves around making complex systems simple.

In the world of SAP consulting, I learned that most "solutions" are just adding more layers of complexity to problems that shouldn't exist. I prefer the opposite approach: stripping everything back until you have a system that actually functions predictably.

"The best code is the code you don't have to explain."

I spend my time building backend pipelines and designing data structures that are reliable. I believe in writing software that serves a clear business purpose rather than chasing trends.
