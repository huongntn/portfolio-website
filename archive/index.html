Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) ↗

Programme Reform of Vocational Training

The Challenge: Facilitating a large-scale digital transformation at LILAMA 2 College, migrating traditional vocational pedagogy to modern E-Learning frameworks.

My Approach: Operated as a Project Assistant to the Digital Ambassador, executing a multi-faceted digital strategy:
• Instructional Architecture: Designed comprehensive training curricula and migrated learning content to atingi.org (Moodle), ensuring cross-language accessibility for Vietnamese faculty.
• Technical & Hardware Engineering: Engineered a custom LED light board for high-production video training, achieving a 40% cost reduction compared to commercial solutions.
• Strategic Consulting: Advised the Directorate of Technical and Vocational Training (DVET) on e-learning policy frameworks to standardize digital education practices.

The Impact: Equipped faculty with sustainable E-Learning competencies, optimized departmental budget allocation through hardware innovation, and established a scalable policy foundation for vocational digital transformation.
