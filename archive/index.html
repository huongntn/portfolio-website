Data Migration Project: SAP and Coupa

The Challenge: Connecting SAP and the Coupa platform to manage master data for Fast Retailing, the Japanese retail giant behind global brands like Uniqlo, GU, and Theory. The goal was to move complex financial and vendor data securely while ensuring it remained accurate and usable in the new system. This initial phase was treated as a trial project to prove our technical capabilities and the effectiveness of our migration strategy.

My Approach: I focused on building the bridge between the two platforms. I developed a custom data migration tool using VBA in Excel, which communicated with Coupa through its official APIs and secure OAuth 2.0 authentication. Using this tool, I migrated critical master data from SAP to Coupa, including supplier profiles, contact information, charts of accounts, tax identifiers, bank details, payment methods, vendor classification codes, purchase organization data, company codes, and operational address structures. Beyond the technical development, I prepared detailed training manuals and step-by-step guidelines to ensure the inhouse teams could operate the migration tool effectively and manage the data independently.

The Impact: I successfully streamlined the data transfer process between SAP and Coupa, reducing the risk of manual errors and saving significant time for the finance department. The successful execution of this trial project demonstrated the reliability of the system, which directly led Fast Retailing to sign a long-term contract, a significant win that solidified our partnership. By creating clear documentation and a functional tool, I ensured that the client’s team could handle their vendor information with greater reliability and security moving forward.

The Impact: I successfully migrated 108 legacy objects to a modern standard, significantly improving system reliability. By maintaining clear documentation and consistent testing, I helped ensure the transition was smooth, which strengthened my ability to handle large-scale technical projects and set a solid foundation for my professional growth in software development.
