Programme Reform of Vocational Training

The Challenge: Leading the transition of LILAMA 2 College’s vocational training from traditional methods to a functional E-Learning framework.

My Approach: As a Project Assistant to the Digital Ambassador, I managed the technical setup of our learning ecosystem. I tailored curricula and training materials for Vietnamese vocational teachers, creating a mix of tutorial videos, on-site interactive sessions, and Moodle-based modules on Atingi. To address budget constraints, I engineered a custom LED light board, which reduced video production hardware costs by 40%. Additionally, I researched cloud solutions to find a system that met both the college’s operational demands and budget, while also working with the DVET to help define the guidelines needed to standardize these digital training practices.

The Impact: I built a reliable system for digital instruction, improved hardware budget efficiency through custom engineering, and helped create the necessary structure for the college to continue its digital transition independently.
